# My customer paid but I don't see it

Most payments show up quickly. If one hasn't, work through these checks. If it still isn't there, send us the details below and we will trace it.

## Steps

1. Open **Wallet**, then **Transaction History**. Clear the filters and search by amount.
2. Check the **Settlements** tab in your Wallet.
3. Ask your customer to confirm they used the exact details in **Accounts**, included the reference if there is one, and that the transfer shows as completed in their bank app.
4. For a payment link or invoice, check whether it shows a payment in **Payment Links** or **Invoices**.

## Good to know

- To trace it, send us: the email on your business account, the amount and currency, the date and time, the sender's name and bank, how they paid and the reference used, and a screenshot of the receipt.
- One message is enough. We'll update you in the same chat.
