# A payment link or invoice isn't working

If a payment link or invoice won't open or can't be paid, check these first.

## Steps

1. For a link: open **Payment Links** and check its status. **Expired** means the time limit passed, so create a new link. **Inactive** means it is switched off.
2. For an invoice: open **Invoices** and check its status. **Draft** means it hasn't been sent yet, so save it and email or download it. Check the client's email address is right.

## If something goes wrong

### Still stuck?

Send us the link or invoice number, the email on your business account, the date and time of the attempt, and a screenshot of what your customer saw.
