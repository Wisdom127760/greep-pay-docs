# Welcome to Greep Pay for business

![Greep Pay for business](banners/banner-welcome.svg)

Greep Pay for business is a dashboard where you get paid, sell online and manage your money, all in one place.

## New here? Start with these

1. [Sign in or create an account](get-started/sign-in-or-create-an-account.md)
2. [Set up your business profile](get-started/set-up-your-business-profile.md)
3. [A tour of your dashboard](get-started/a-tour-of-your-dashboard.md)
4. [Receive money by bank transfer](get-paid/receive-money-by-bank-transfer.md)

## What you can do

- [**Get started**](get-started/README.md): Sign in, set up your business and find your way around the dashboard.
- [**Get paid**](get-paid/README.md): Share your bank details, send payment links and bill clients with invoices.
- [**Your money**](your-money/README.md): See your balances, put money aside and set your exchange rates.
- [**Sell online**](sell-online/README.md): Add products, open your shop and share links that bring customers in.

Need help? Press **Help** in your dashboard to contact our support team.
