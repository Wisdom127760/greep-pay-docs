# Your wallet

## What you see

Open **Wallet**. At the top are four figures:

- **Ledger balance**
- **Available balance**
- **Pending balance**
- **Total transactions**

Below them are two tabs: **Transaction History** and **Settlements**.

[confirm: what each balance means, in one line each]

## Find a transaction

1. Open **Transaction History**.
2. Use the filters to narrow it down: **All types**, **All statuses**, or search for an amount or name.
3. Each row shows the transaction, its type, the amount, the method and its status.
4. Press **Export** to download your history.

## Receive or send money

Use **Receive** to ask a customer to pay you. See [Request money from your wallet](../get-paid/request-money-from-your-wallet.md).
