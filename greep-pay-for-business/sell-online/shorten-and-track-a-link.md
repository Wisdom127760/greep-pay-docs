# Shorten and track a link

A short link is easier to share than a long one, and it shows you how many people use it.

## Steps

1. Open **Link Shortener** and press **Shorten New Link**.
2. Paste the destination URL, then give it a title or campaign name.
3. Optional: enter a custom alias for the end of the link. Leave it empty to get an automatic code.
4. Press the button to create the short link.

## Good to know

- The page shows your total short links, clicks, unique visitors and active links. Filter by All Links, Active or Recently Added.
- You can also shorten your store link from Storefront.
