# List your business on Greep Discover

Greep Discover is an app aimed at people abroad. A listing puts your business in front of them.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Integrations**.
2. Under Discovery, find **Greep Discover**. It shows **Connected** once your business is listed.
3. Press **Manage** to change your listing.
