# Make a Link-in-bio page

A Link-in-bio page gives you one link to put in your social profile, with everything you want to share on it.

## Steps

1. Open **Link-in-bio**.
2. Choose your **page address** (3 to 50 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens).
3. Under Profile Info, upload your logo and add your name and a short description.
4. Choose a page theme and text colour, then press **Add block** to add your links.
5. Press **Save changes**. Use **Copy link** or **Share** to get your page's address, and **Unpublish** to take it down.
