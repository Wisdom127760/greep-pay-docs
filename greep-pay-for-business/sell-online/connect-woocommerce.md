# Connect WooCommerce

Connect your WooCommerce store to sync inventory and orders and accept Greep Pay at checkout.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Integrations**.
2. Under E-commerce platforms, find **WooCommerce** and press **Connect**.
3. Follow the steps on screen.
