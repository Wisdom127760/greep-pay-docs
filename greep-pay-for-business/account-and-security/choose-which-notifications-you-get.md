# Choose which notifications you get

You choose which updates you hear about and how.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**.
2. Switch on the updates you want. **Transactions:** successful, failed, large transactions and pending approvals. **Orders:** new orders, payment received and order completed.
3. Under **Notification Channels**, choose **Email**, **SMS** or **Push notification**.
