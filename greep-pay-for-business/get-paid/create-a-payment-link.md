# Create a payment link

A payment link lets you collect a fixed amount without sending bank details or an invoice.

## Steps

1. Open **Payment Links**, then press **Create New Link**.
2. Choose the **currency** and type the **amount**, or tap a quick amount.
3. Give the link a **name**, for example "Yoga Class Registration".
4. Optional: turn on **Link Expiration** and choose a date and time. After that the link stops working.
5. Press **Generate Link** and share it with your customer.

## Good to know

- The Payment Links page shows your total links, active links, clicks and payments. Filter by All, Active, Inactive or Expired.
