# Change your password or transaction PIN

You can change your password and your transaction PIN yourself in Settings.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Account Profile**.
2. Under **Security**, find **Change Password** or **Change Transaction PIN** and press **Edit**.

## Good to know

- Your transaction PIN is used to approve sensitive transactions.
- Never share your password, PIN or secret API key with anyone, including our support team. We will never ask for them.
