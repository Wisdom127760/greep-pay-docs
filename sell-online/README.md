# Sell online

![Sell online](../.gitbook/assets/banner-sell-online.svg)

Add products, open your shop and share links that bring customers in.

## In this section

- [**Add a product**](add-a-product.md): Add products to your store, as a draft or ready to sell.
- [**Set up your storefront**](set-up-your-storefront.md): Open your own online shop with a public link.
- [**Set up shipping**](set-up-shipping.md): Let customers choose delivery, with rates worked out automatically.
- [**Make a Link-in-bio page**](make-a-link-in-bio-page.md): One page that holds all your links.
- [**Shorten and track a link**](shorten-and-track-a-link.md): Make a short link and see how many people click it.
- [**Connect WooCommerce**](connect-woocommerce.md): Sync your WooCommerce store with Greep Pay.
- [**List your business on Greep Discover**](list-your-business-on-greep-discover.md): Show your business on Greep Discover, the app for people abroad.
