# Add a product

Add the things you sell. You can save a product as a draft and publish it when you're ready.

## Steps

1. Open **Products**, then press **Add Product**. The form has 5 steps.
2. In step 1, fill in the **product name**, **description** and **category**. These three are required.
3. Optional: add an SKU, choose the product type (Physical or Digital) and add tags.
4. Continue through the steps. At any step press **Save as Draft** to keep working later, or **Publish Product**.

## Good to know

- Drafts are not shown to customers until you publish them.
- The Products page shows total, active, sold-out and draft products. Filter by All, Active, Draft or Sold Out, and search by name or SKU.

## If something goes wrong

### It won't let me go to the next step

Make sure the product name, description and category are all filled in, then press Next Step again.
