# Set up shipping

Auto Shipping works out shipping rates and handles deliveries through Greep Pay's shipping partners.

## Before you start

- You need at least one validated pickup address.

## Steps

1. Open **Storefront**, then the **Shipping** tab.
2. Add and validate your pickup address.
3. Switch **Auto Shipping** on, then press **Save**.

## Good to know

- Auto Shipping is off until you turn it on.
