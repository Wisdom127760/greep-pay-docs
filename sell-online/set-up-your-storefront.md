# Set up your storefront

Your storefront is your public shop. Customers visit its link to see your products.

## Steps

1. Open **Storefront**. The page shows whether your store is **Live**.
2. Under **General Details**, add a cover photo (SVG, PNG, JPG or GIF, up to 5MB).
3. Enter your store name and a short description.
4. Press **Save**.

## Good to know

- Your store's public link is shown at the bottom of the page.
- To show or hide Storefront in your sidebar, use **Settings**, then **Tools**.
