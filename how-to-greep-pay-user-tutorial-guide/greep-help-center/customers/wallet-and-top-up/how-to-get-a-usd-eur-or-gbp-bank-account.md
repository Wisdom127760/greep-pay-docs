# How to Get a USD, EUR, or GBP Bank Account

Use this feature to open a local bank account in **USD**, **EUR**, or **GBP** and receive payments worldwide.

Personalized bank accounts are available for **$4.99**.

#### What you get

* A local account in **USD**, **EUR**, or **GBP**.
* A bank account you can use to receive payments.
* Access to account details inside the app.

{% hint style="info" %}
Personalized bank accounts cost **$4.99**.

This option is available when you choose to open a bank account in the app.
{% endhint %}

#### How to get a bank account

**Step 1: Open the Account section**

* From the Home screen, open **Account**.

**Step 2: Choose your currency**

* Select **USD**, **EUR**, or **GBP**.
* Review the account options available for that currency.

**Step 3: Get your account**

* Tap **"Get for $4.99"** if you want a personalized account.
* Follow the prompts to complete setup.

**Step 4: View your bank details**

* Open the created account to see your bank details.
* Copy the details exactly as shown before you share or use them.

#### USD account details and transfer rules

If you open a **USD** account, it supports **ACH** and **wire transfers**.

* The account type is **Checking**.
* If a local US address is required, you can use the bank address shown for the account.
* Individual third-party deposits must be **less than $4,000**.
* Third-party deposits from business accounts have **no limit**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A third-party deposit is a payment sent by someone else into your account.

Review the transfer type and limits before sharing your USD account details.
{% endhint %}

#### ACH transfers

* ACH is used for local US bank transfers.
* ACH deposits can take **1–2 business days**.
* ACH receiving fees are **0.8%**, with a minimum of **$2** and a maximum of **$10**.
* Use the **ACH routing number** for ACH transfers.

#### Wire transfers

* Fedwire transfers usually arrive within **1 business day**.
* Fedwire receiving fees are a flat **$20**.
* Use the **wire routing number** for wire transfers.

#### Important for USD wire deposits

When you fund through **USD Bank Transfer**, include your unique **Deposit Reference** in the memo or reference field.

You can find this reference in the app under **Fund Wallet** → **Bank Transfer**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you do not include your **Deposit Reference**, your funds will **not** be credited to your account.
{% endhint %}
