# Customer Support

Get help from Greep Pay through our official support channels.

Use **WhatsApp**, **email**, or our official **social media accounts** to reach the team.

{% hint style="info" %}
Choose the channel that matches your issue for faster help.
{% endhint %}

### Open customer support

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open **Profile**

Go to your account menu.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select **Customer Support**

Open the support page to see the available contact options.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Support channels

#### 💬 WhatsApp support

Use the official Greep Pay [WhatsApp number](https://wa.me/+905391223042) for direct support conversations.

This is useful for urgent issues, payment questions, and account help.

{% hint style="success" %}
Use WhatsApp for the fastest response on urgent issues, bugs and other related matters.
{% endhint %}

#### 📧 Email support

Use the official support [email](mailto:greeppay@gmail.com) when you want to send full details or attachments.

This is useful for account issues, disputes, and follow-up requests.

#### 🌐 Social media support

You can also contact Greep Pay through official social media accounts.

Use this option for general help, updates, and public support requests.

#### 💡 Community feedback channel

Join the official [Telegram group](https://t.me/+QCLLvRXDLdpkYjA0) to share product ideas and suggestions.

Use this channel to share feature ideas, UX/UI feedback, and comments on how to improve the platform.

The group is open to outside developers, designers, and anyone with useful feedback.

### Choose the right channel

* **WhatsApp** for urgent help and payment issues
* **Email** for detailed reports and attachments
* **Telegram** for ideas, UX/UI feedback, and improvement suggestions

### What to include when you contact support

For faster assistance, always provide:

* Your registered email or phone number
* Transaction ID (if applicable)
* Screenshot of the issue
* Detailed description of the problem
* Date and time of the issue
* Steps you've already tried

{% hint style="warning" %}
Do not share sensitive information you do not need for support.
{% endhint %}

### Common support cases

#### 🔐 Forgot transaction PIN

Contact support through WhatsApp, email, or official social media.

Be ready to verify your identity before a reset.

#### ⏳ Transaction pending

Wait a few minutes and check your transaction history.

If the payment is still pending, contact support and include the transaction ID.

#### 💸 Wrong amount sent

Contact support as soon as possible with the full transaction details.

Do not make extra transactions while the issue is being reviewed.

#### 🔓 Account access issues

Contact support and explain the access problem clearly.

The team may ask you to confirm your identity before helping further.

### What happens next

Once support reviews your issue, the team will guide you on the next step through the same channel.
