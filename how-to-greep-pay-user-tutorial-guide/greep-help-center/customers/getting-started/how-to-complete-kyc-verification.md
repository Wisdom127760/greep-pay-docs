#### Steps

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Tap **KYC levels**
3. Choose the verification level you want to upgrade to
4. Enter your personal details
5. Upload the required verification documents:
   * A valid ID, such as Passport or NIN
   * A selfie for face verification
6. Submit for review

{% hint style="info" %}
KYC review usually takes a few minutes to 24 hours. If it takes longer, contact support.
{% endhint %}

### Complete your selfie verification

1. Select **Selfie Verification**
2. Allow camera access when prompted
3. Position your face within the frame shown on your screen
   * Make sure your full face is visible
4. Look directly into the camera
5. **Smile naturally** when prompted
6. Hold still while the app captures your selfie
7. Wait for confirmation or proceed if additional steps are required

### After your KYC verification is approved

* You will receive an email confirmation
* Your account will be upgraded to Tier 2
* You can access more features, including wallet funding and your virtual card

***

{% hint style="success" %}
**Tips for successful identity verification:**

* Make sure photos are clear and well-lit
* All four corners of your document must be visible
* No glare or blur
* Selfie must match your document photo
{% endhint %}
