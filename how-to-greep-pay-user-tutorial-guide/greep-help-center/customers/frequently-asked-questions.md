# Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about GreepPay.

Use this page for product basics, verification, safety, and account rules.

**Related terms:** GreepPay, KYC, Verification, Virtual Card, Wallet Top-Up, Savings, Safety, Referrals

### General

<details>

<summary>What is GreepPay?</summary>

GreepPay is a cross-border payments platform that lets you spend your currency globally.

You can get a free virtual card, top up your wallet, send and receive money internationally, earn rewards, and shop in the app.

For more detail, see [Introduction](introduction.md), [Savings](savings/), [Virtual Card](virtual-card/), and [Marketplace & Events](marketplace-and-events.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Which countries does GreepPay support?</summary>

GreepPay primarily serves users in Northern Cyprus and the African diaspora.

Supported corridors include Nigeria (**NGN**), Kenya (**KES**), Ghana (**GHS**), Cameroon (**XAF**), Tanzania (**TZS**), and more.

Supported routes expand regularly.

</details>

### Safety and compliance

<details>

<summary>Is GreepPay safe?</summary>

Yes.

P2P transactions are protected by GreepPay's **Secure Escrow** system.

Your card and account are protected by your **PIN**, **passcode**, and supported biometric authentication.

GreepPay works with compliant third-party partners that handle key payment rails.

Product safety also depends on internal controls, operating processes, and partner reviews.

See [Platform security and infrastructure](security-and-settings/platform-security-and-infrastructure.md) for more detail.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Which cloud provider does GreepPay use?</summary>

GreepPay runs on **Microsoft Azure** cloud infrastructure.

Azure is the hosting layer used for core application services and supporting workloads.

See [Platform security and infrastructure](security-and-settings/platform-security-and-infrastructure.md) for more detail.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does GreepPay support gambling transactions?</summary>

No.

GreepPay does not support gambling deposits or withdrawals.

This is a compliance and regulatory requirement.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use GreepPay from Sudan or another restricted country?</summary>

Cards cannot be issued to users who reside in sanctioned regions.

If you are a national of a restricted country but live elsewhere, you can submit a valid **Resident Permit** from your current country of residence to the support team for review.

Use [Customer Support](customer-support.md) if you need help with this review.

</details>

### Verification

<details>

<summary>How long does KYC take?</summary>

KYC is usually completed within minutes.

In some cases, it can take up to 24 hours.

You receive an email when your KYC is approved or when more action is needed.

See [How to complete KYC verification](getting-started/how-to-complete-kyc-verification.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What documents do I need for KYC?</summary>

GreepPay accepts a valid **International Passport**, **National ID Card**, or **Driver's License**.

Your document must be valid and not expired.

</details>

### Virtual card and payments

<details>

<summary>Why is my card failing on Netflix, iCloud, or other subscriptions?</summary>

This usually happens because of a billing country mismatch.

Greep Pay virtual cards are **US-based** cards.

Some merchants check whether your card country matches the billing country on your account.

If your Netflix, iCloud, or store billing country is set to another country, the payment can fail.

Try this:

1. Open the billing settings for the merchant or platform.
2. Set the billing country to **United States**.
3. Re-enter your Greep Pay card details.
4. Try the payment again.

If the payment still fails, contact [Customer Support](customer-support.md) with the merchant name and a screenshot of the error.

For more checks, see [Troubleshooting: Card declined](virtual-card/troubleshooting-card-declined.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why was I charged after a declined card payment?</summary>

Some failed card attempts still create a processing cost.

Our card processing partner can charge us even when the payment does not go through.

Your first declined card transaction each calendar month is FREE.

After that, later declined attempts can incur a **$0.50 decline fee**.

This fee helps cover the cost of failed card attempts.

It is not a charge on successful payments.

Automatic retries from subscription services can also trigger declined attempts.

If you need help checking a decline fee, contact [Customer Support](customer-support.md) with the merchant name and date of the attempt.

</details>

### Account rules

<details>

<summary>Can I have more than one GreepPay account?</summary>

No.

Each person can only have one GreepPay account.

Creating multiple accounts violates the terms of service.

</details>

### Rewards and referrals

<details>

<summary>When does a referral activate?</summary>

A referral activates after the invited user gets their virtual card.

Using the referral link during sign-up is still required.

Sign-up alone does not complete the referral requirement.

</details>

### Savings

<details>

<summary>What is a Savings Pot?</summary>

A Savings Pot is a dedicated space in your Greep Pay wallet for saving toward a goal or setting money aside.

You can create multiple pots and manage each one separately.

See [Savings](savings/).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the difference between Flex, Goal, and Vault?</summary>

**Flex** is for more flexible saving with easier access to funds.

**Goal** is for saving toward a target amount.

**Vault** is for locking funds for a set period.

See [Savings Pot Types Explained](savings/savings-pot-types-explained.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I create a Savings Pot?</summary>

Open **Savings**, tap **Create Pot**, choose your pot type, add your pot details, and fund it from your **Greep Pay wallet balance**.

See [How to Create a Savings Pot](savings/how-to-create-a-savings-pot.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use auto-save with Savings Pots?</summary>

Yes, if auto-save options are available for your pot.

You may see **Percentage of Payment** or **Round-Up Payments** in the setup flow or pot settings.

See [How Auto-Save Works in Savings Pots](savings/how-auto-save-works-in-savings-pots.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I withdraw from a locked Savings Pot?</summary>

Withdrawals can be restricted if the pot is locked.

Access depends on the pot rules shown in the app.

See [How to Manage a Savings Pot](savings/how-to-manage-a-savings-pot.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does every Savings Pot earn the same rate?</summary>

Rates and availability can vary by pot type and current product terms.

Check the app for the rate shown on your selected pot.

</details>
