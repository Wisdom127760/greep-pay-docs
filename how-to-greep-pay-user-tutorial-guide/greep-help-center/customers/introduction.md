# Introduction

**GreepPay is a cross-border payments platform built for the African diaspora.**

GreepPay makes it simple to move money globally.

Use GreepPay to **top up your wallet**, **send money internationally**, **receive money**, pay online with a **virtual card**, and manage cross-border payments from one app.

GreepPay supports users across Northern Cyprus and the African diaspora.

The platform is built for people who need faster and more flexible **international payments**, including students, workers, founders, and families sending money across borders.

With GreepPay, you can trade local currency through the **P2P agent network**, fund your balance with fiat or crypto, and spend globally with a digital wallet and virtual card.

Supported markets include Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Cameroon, Tanzania, and other growing African payment corridors.

We started in Northern Cyprus and continue to expand across Africa and its diaspora communities.

We believe your location should never limit how far your money can go.

### What GreepPay helps you do

Use GreepPay to:

* send and receive cross-border payments
* top up a wallet with bank transfer, card payment, P2P, or cryptocurrency
* create a virtual card for online payments and subscriptions
* withdraw money to supported bank accounts, mobile money, or crypto wallets
* shop in the marketplace and buy event tickets in the app

### Why people use GreepPay

GreepPay combines **cross-border transfers**, **multi-currency wallets**, **virtual cards**, and **P2P exchange** in one product.

This helps users manage global payments without switching between multiple apps or services.
