# Savings Pot FAQ

Find quick answers to common questions about **Savings Pots** in Greep Pay.

{% include "../../../.gitbook/includes/savings-related-terms.md" %}

<details>

<summary>What is a Savings Pot?</summary>

A Savings Pot is a dedicated space in your Greep Pay wallet for saving toward a goal or setting money aside.

You can create more than one pot and track each one separately.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the difference between Flex, Goal, and Vault?</summary>

**Flex** is for flexible saving with easier access to funds.

**Goal** is for saving toward a target amount.

**Vault** is for locking funds for a set period.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I fund a Savings Pot?</summary>

Open **Savings**, select or create a pot, tap **Fund Pot** or **Add Money**, then confirm the transfer from your **Greep Pay wallet balance**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I withdraw from a locked pot?</summary>

Withdrawals can be restricted if the pot is locked.

Access usually depends on the pot rules shown in the app.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use auto-save on my Savings Pot?</summary>

Yes, if auto-save options are available for your pot.

You may see **Percentage of Payment** or **Round-Up Payments** in the setup flow or pot settings.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does every Savings Pot earn the same rate?</summary>

Rates and availability can vary by pot type and current product terms.

Check the app for the rate shown on your selected pot.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Where can I get help with Savings Pots?</summary>

If you need help, contact [Customer Support](../customer-support.md).

</details>
