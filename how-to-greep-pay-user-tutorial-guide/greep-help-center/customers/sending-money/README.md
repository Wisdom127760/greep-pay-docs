# Sending Money

Send money from Greep Pay using the transfer method that fits your recipient.

You can send money to another Greep Pay user or transfer money to a bank account through P2P.

**Related terms:** Send Money, Greep Tag, Username, Beneficiary, Bank Transfer, P2P Transfer, Wallet Payment

### Ways to send money with Greep Pay

#### Transfer to another Greep Pay user

Use this option for instant in-app transfers between Greep Pay accounts.

Send money with a Greep tag, username, or saved beneficiary.

Read [Transfer to GreepPay Users](transfer-to-greeppay-users.md).

#### Transfer to a bank account through P2P

Use P2P transfer when you want to send money to a bank account.

You can compare verified trader offers, review rates, and complete the transfer flow in app.

Read [Transfer to Bank Account (P2P)](transfer-to-bank-account-p2p.md).

### Before you send money

Before you confirm any transfer:

* check the recipient details carefully
* review the amount, fees, and exchange rate if shown
* make sure your wallet balance is enough

### Choose the right transfer method

Use **Greep Pay transfer** for instant user-to-user payments.

Use **P2P bank transfer** for supported bank payouts.
