# Cross-border fees explained

Cross-border fees can apply when you use your Greep Pay virtual card for international payments.

These charges are common when a merchant is based in another country or when a payment is processed in a different currency.

{% hint style="info" %}
Cross-border fees are different from the purchase amount itself.

Some charges can appear instantly or shortly after the original card transaction.
{% endhint %}

### When cross-border fees can happen

You can see cross-border or foreign transaction fees when:

* the merchant is outside the card's billing region
* the payment is processed in a foreign currency
* the card network or payment processor adds international charges

### Why your final card charge can differ

The amount you first see may not always match the final settled amount.

This can happen because of:

* currency conversion rates
* cross-border processing fees
* delayed settlement from the merchant or card network

### How to avoid payment issues

Before you make an international card payment:

* keep extra balance on your virtual card
* check the payment currency at checkout
* review the merchant location when possible

{% hint style="warning" %}
Always keep a small extra balance on your card.

This helps cover foreign transaction fees, exchange rate changes, or delayed charges after payment.
{% endhint %}
