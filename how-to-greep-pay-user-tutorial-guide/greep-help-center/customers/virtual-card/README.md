# Virtual Card

### About the Greep Pay virtual card

The Greep Pay virtual card is a digital payment card for secure online payments, subscriptions, and international transactions.

You can fund it directly from your Greep Pay wallet and use it on supported websites and apps worldwide.

Greep Pay offers virtual cards in **Visa** and **Mastercard**, so you can pay online without carrying a physical card.

**Related terms:** Virtual Card, Card Activation, Fund Card, Card Details, Freeze Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Card Declined

#### What you can do with a virtual card

* Pay for online shopping and in-app purchases
* Manage subscriptions like streaming and software services
* Shop internationally with better control over spending

#### Why use the Greep Pay virtual card

* Fund your card instantly from your wallet
* Freeze or unfreeze your card when needed
* Track card activity and transactions in real time
