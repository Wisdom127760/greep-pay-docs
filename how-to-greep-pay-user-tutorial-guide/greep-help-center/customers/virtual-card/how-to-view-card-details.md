# How to view card details

View your Greep Pay virtual card details from the **Cards** tab.

This lets you check your card number, expiry date, and CVV when you need them for supported online payments.

{% hint style="info" %}
Keep your card details private.

Only view or copy them when you are making a payment on a trusted website or app.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the Cards tab

From the bottom navigation, tap **Cards**.

Select the virtual card you want to check.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Tap Details

On the card screen, tap **Details**.

Your card information opens on the next screen.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Review your card information

Check the details you need, such as:

* Card number
* Expiry date
* CVV
* Cardholder name

Use these details only on supported merchants and secure checkout pages.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Hide your details when you are done

Close the details view after you finish.

If your card supports a hide or visibility toggle, use it to protect your information on screen.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

If your card balance is low, read [How to fund your card](how-to-fund-your-card.md).

If a payment fails, check [Troubleshooting: Card declined](troubleshooting-card-declined.md).
