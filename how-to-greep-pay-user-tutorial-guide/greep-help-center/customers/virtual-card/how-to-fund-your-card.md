# How to fund your card

Add money to your Greep Pay virtual card from the **Cards** tab.

Funding your virtual card lets you pay for online purchases, subscriptions, and other supported card transactions.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Your wallet balance must be higher than the amount you want to add.

This helps cover your card funding amount and any applicable fees.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open your virtual card

Go to the **Cards** tab in the app.

Select the virtual card you want to fund.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-05-15 at 12.19.02 PM.png" alt="Virtual card screen with the Add Money button" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Tap Add Money

On the card screen, tap **Add Money**.

This opens the card top-up flow from your wallet.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter the funding amount

Enter the amount you want to move from your wallet to your virtual card.

Review the amount before you continue.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Confirm the card top-up

Confirm the transfer to fund your card.

After the transfer succeeds, refresh the page if needed to see the updated card balance.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

Once your card is funded, you can use it for online payments and subscriptions on supported merchants.

If you need your card information, read [How to view card details](how-to-view-card-details.md).
