# Troubleshooting: Card declined

If your Greep Pay virtual card is declined, the payment usually fails because of balance, card status, merchant support, or verification issues.

Use these checks to fix a failed card payment.

{% hint style="info" %}
Some declined payments still create pending charges or reversals.

Check your card balance and recent transactions before you try again.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Some merchants retry failed card payments automatically.

Your first declined card transaction each calendar month is free.

After that, later declined attempts can incur a **$0.50 decline fee**.

Avoid repeated retries until you confirm your balance, card status, and billing details.
{% endhint %}

### Common reasons your virtual card is declined

Your payment can fail if:

* your card balance is too low
* your card is locked or frozen

{% hint style="warning" %}
This is a **US-based virtual card**.

If you are adding it to **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**, your device region or wallet region may need to be set to the **United States**.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Check your card balance

Open the **Cards** tab and select your virtual card.

Make sure your available balance is enough to cover the full payment and any extra fees.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check if the card is locked

On the card screen, confirm that your card is not frozen.

If it is locked, unfreeze it before you try the payment again.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Review your card details

If you entered the card manually, check the card number, expiry date, CVV, and cardholder name.

Wrong details can cause an instant card decline.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check for international or cross-border charges

Some merchants add foreign transaction fees or settle in a different currency.

Keep extra balance on the card to cover these charges.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Try the payment again

After you confirm your balance, card status, and payment details, retry the transaction.

If the payment still fails, try another supported merchant or payment method.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Card payment dos and don'ts

#### Do

* Check your card balance and payment preview before you confirm a payment.
* Use your card on merchants that clearly support virtual cards. Some platforms can reject certain BIN ranges.
* Enter your PIN carefully. Repeated wrong attempts can trigger a decline.
* Contact support if a transaction declines and the reason is unclear. Decline codes can vary.
* Report it immediately if your card shows as terminated but you still see charge attempts or declines. That needs escalation.

#### Don't

* Do not retry a declined payment repeatedly before you confirm the cause.
* Do not use the card for very small payments if you want to minimize fees.
* Do not assume a rejected transaction means your account has an issue. Some platforms reject specific BIN ranges on their side.
* Do not share your card details or PIN with anyone, including anyone claiming to be Greep Pay support.
* Do not ignore repeated declines. After your first declined card transaction in a calendar month, later declined attempts can incur a **$0.50 decline fee**.
