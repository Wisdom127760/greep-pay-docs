# Payment Methods and Currencies

Greep Checkout helps your business accept payments in the ways customers prefer.

### Supported payment options

Customers can pay using supported local African currencies, global currencies, and major cryptocurrencies.

This lets you serve customers across African markets and the diaspora through one checkout experience.

### Local and global currencies

Greep Checkout supports payments in more than 25 local African and global currencies. Customers can pay in currencies they know and use.

You do not need a separate payment provider for every market you serve.

### Cryptocurrency payments

Greep Checkout also supports major cryptocurrencies. This gives crypto-native customers another way to pay.

Your business does not need to manage customer crypto wallets or handle conversions directly.

### Choosing the right options

Offer payment options that match your customers and markets. Keep the price and payment details clear before checkout.
