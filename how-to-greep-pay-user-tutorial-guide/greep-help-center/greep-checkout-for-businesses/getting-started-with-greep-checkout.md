# Getting Started with Greep Checkout

Use Greep Checkout to accept supported local currencies, global currencies, and cryptocurrency.

### Before you begin

Decide which products, services, or invoices you want customers to pay for. Confirm the customer markets you plan to serve.

Greep Checkout supports cross-border and diaspora payments through one payment experience.

### Plan your checkout experience

Keep the amount, product description, and payment instructions clear. Customers should know what they are paying for before checkout.

Offer the supported payment options that fit your customers. This can reduce payment barriers and checkout drop-off.

### What customers see

Customers complete payment in a secure, isolated checkout experience. They can select from supported local currencies, global currencies, or cryptocurrency.

Review the [greep-checkout.md](greep-checkout.md "mention") to understand the customer and business experience.

### Need help?

Contact business support before launch if you need help with a checkout question.
