# Exchange rates

This is for businesses that exchange currencies. You set the rates you offer for each currency pair.

## Steps

1. Open **Exchange Rates**. Your offers are listed with their pair, type (Buy or Sell), rate, minimum, maximum and status.
2. Press **Add Currency Pair**.
3. Choose the currency to list (for example NGN). It is quoted against another currency (for example USDC). The page shows the current reference mid-market rate to help you.
4. Press **Continue** and set your rates and limits.
