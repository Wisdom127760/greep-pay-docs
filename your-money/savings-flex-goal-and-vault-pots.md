# Savings: Flex, Goal and Vault pots

A savings pot keeps part of your money aside for a purpose.

## Steps

1. Open **Savings** and press **Create New Savings**.
2. Choose the type: **Flex** (withdraw anytime), **Goal** (save towards an amount) or **Vault** (lock funds for a time).
3. Enter a goal name and, if asked, a target amount. You can add an image (PNG, JPG or WEBP, up to 2MB).
4. Press **Create pot**.

## Good to know

- Your Savings page shows total savings, monthly interest earned and your active goals. Each pot shows how much you've saved and your progress.
