# Business FAQ

### Who is Greep Checkout for?

Greep Checkout is for businesses selling products, services, tickets, or invoices to customers across markets.

It is especially useful for businesses serving African customers, diaspora customers, or crypto-native customers.

### Which payment options can customers use?

Customers can use supported local African currencies, global currencies, and major cryptocurrencies.

### Can I serve more than one market?

Yes. Greep Checkout supports payments in more than 25 local African and global currencies through one checkout experience.

### Do I need separate payment providers for each country?

No. Greep Checkout helps you accept payments across multiple markets without separate providers for each country.

### Do I need to manage customer crypto wallets?

No. Greep Checkout handles the payment experience. Your business does not manage customer wallets or conversions directly.
