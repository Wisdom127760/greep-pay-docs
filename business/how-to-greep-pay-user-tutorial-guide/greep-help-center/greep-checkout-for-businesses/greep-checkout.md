# Greep Checkout

Greep Checkout connects customers and businesses through one secure payment experience. Customers pay with their preferred supported method. Businesses accept payments across markets without separate payment setups.

### Watch the checkout walkthrough

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/QYe7ZuTkDJ4" %}

### For customers

Use Greep Checkout when a business offers it at payment.

#### Pay with your preferred supported method

Choose from supported local and global currencies, or supported cryptocurrency. Review the payment details before you confirm.

#### Pay with confidence

Checkout runs in a secure, isolated payment experience. You can complete a purchase without changing how you normally pay.

### For businesses

Use Greep Checkout to accept payments from customers in African markets, globally, and across the diaspora.

#### Accept more payment options

Offer customers supported local currencies, global currencies, and major cryptocurrencies through one checkout. This reduces payment barriers and helps more customers complete purchases.

#### Reach more markets with one setup

Accept payments in more than 25 local African and global currencies. You do not need separate bank accounts or payment providers for each country.

This supports cross-border sales while reducing operational overhead.

#### Accept crypto payments without extra complexity

Serve crypto-native customers alongside customers who use traditional currencies. Greep Checkout handles the payment experience, so you do not manage crypto wallets or conversions yourself.

### Common business use cases

Greep Checkout suits businesses that:

* sell products or services across borders
* sell event tickets internationally
* collect invoices from customers in different markets

One secure checkout helps you serve more customers while keeping payments simple.
