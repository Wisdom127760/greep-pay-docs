# Troubleshoot Checkout Payments

Use this guide when a customer reports a problem during checkout.

### Check the payment details first

Ask the customer to confirm the payment amount and the payment option they selected. Confirm the date and time of the attempt.

Ask them not to share passwords, card details, wallet recovery phrases, or other sensitive information.

### Collect useful information

Include these details when you contact support:

* the customer’s payment reference, if available
* the amount, currency, date, and time
* screenshots and a short description of what happened

### Avoid duplicate payments

Do not ask the customer to retry repeatedly. First confirm whether the original payment attempt has completed.

If the issue continues, contact business support with the information above.

### Keep customers informed

Tell the customer their payment is being reviewed. Use the same support channel for any follow-up.
