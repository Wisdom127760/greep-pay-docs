# Transfer to Bank Account (P2P)

Send money to a bank account with the **P2P transfer** flow in Greep Pay.

This option helps you send money through verified traders when you want to complete a bank transfer.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Only scammers will ask you to trade outside Greep Pay.

Always complete your P2P bank transfer inside the app.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the P2P transfer flow

From the home screen, tap **Send**.

Select **Transfer to (Default currency) Account** or **Buy (for example, NGN)**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Browse verified trader offers

Review the list of available P2P offers.

Check the:

* exchange rate
* available amount limits
* trader rating and verification status

Use **Filter** to narrow down the offers.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose a trader

Tap the trader you want to use.

Review the trader terms, then tap **Buy from P2P** or **Continue**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter the bank transfer details

Enter the amount you want to send.

Select or add the recipient bank details:

* bank name
* account number
* account name
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Complete the bank transfer

The trader processes the transfer to the bank account.

Track the status in the chat, then tap **Mark as Complete** or **Confirm Received** when the transfer is done.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Confirm the payout

Verify with the recipient that the bank transfer arrived successfully.

You can then rate the trader and complete the P2P transaction.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
For safer P2P transfers:

* trade only with verified traders
* never share your PIN or password
* report suspicious activity to Customer Support
{% endhint %}

### What happens next

After the transfer is complete, confirm that the recipient received the bank payment.

If you want to send money another way, return to [Sending Money](./).
