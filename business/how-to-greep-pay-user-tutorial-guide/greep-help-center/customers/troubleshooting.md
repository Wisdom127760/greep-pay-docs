# Troubleshooting

Use this page to fix the most common issues in GreepPay.

Start with the issue that matches what you see in the app.

**Related terms:** Card Declined, Top-Up Pending, Withdrawal Issue, KYC Failed, OTP Not Received, Referral Issue, App Error

{% hint style="info" %}
If the steps on this page do not fix the problem, contact [Customer Support](customer-support.md) with the transaction reference, merchant name, or error message.
{% endhint %}

### Card issues

<details>

<summary>My card was declined</summary>

Check these first:

1. Make sure your **card balance** is enough.
2. Confirm the merchant accepts **virtual cards**.
3. Some merchants in **Turkey** or **Northern Cyprus** may not accept virtual cards.
4. Make sure the billing details you entered match the app exactly.
5. If the card is still declined, contact support with the merchant name and error message.

For a full guide, see [Troubleshooting: Card declined](virtual-card/troubleshooting-card-declined.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>My card balance is different from what I expect</summary>

This usually happens when a previously authorised transaction settles later.

That delayed settlement can create a small deduction after the original card activity.

This is not usually fraud or an unauthorised charge.

For a full guide, see [Troubleshooting: Balance mismatch](virtual-card/troubleshooting-balance-mismatch.md).

</details>

### Funding and withdrawal issues

<details>

<summary>My top-up is not showing in my balance</summary>

Check the funding method first:

1. For **P2P**, make sure you tapped **I've Sent The Money** and the agent confirmed receipt.
2. For **crypto**, wait for blockchain confirmations. This can take **5 to 30 minutes**.
3. For **wire transfer**, wait **1 to 3 business days**.
4. If it takes longer than expected, contact support with your transaction reference.

See [How to Fund Your Wallet with P2P](wallet-and-top-up/how-to-fund-your-wallet-with-p2p.md) for the P2P funding flow.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I cannot withdraw my funds</summary>

Check these first:

1. Make sure your **KYC** is complete.
2. Confirm you meet the minimum withdrawal amount.
3. Make sure you selected the correct currency and withdrawal method.
4. If the issue continues, contact support.

See [Withdraw Money](withdraw-money/) and [How to complete KYC verification](getting-started/how-to-complete-kyc-verification.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I sent crypto to the wrong network</summary>

Crypto transactions are irreversible.

If you send **USDT** or **USDC** on an unsupported network, such as **BEP-20/BSC** instead of a supported network, the funds cannot be recovered.

Always confirm the exact network before you send crypto.

See [Withdraw to Crypto Wallet](withdraw-money/withdraw-to-crypto-wallet.md).

</details>

### Verification and access issues

<details>

<summary>I cannot complete KYC</summary>

Check these first:

1. Make sure your photos are clear and well lit.
2. Make sure all four corners of your document are visible.
3. Make sure your selfie matches your document photo.
4. If your document type is not available for your country, contact support.

See [How to complete KYC verification](getting-started/how-to-complete-kyc-verification.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I am not receiving OTP codes</summary>

Try these checks:

1. Check your email **spam** or **junk** folder.
2. If you signed up with an Apple private email, update it to a real address in **Profile** → **Settings**.
3. Make sure your registered phone number is correct.
4. If the issue continues, contact support.

</details>

### App and notification issues

<details>

<summary>The app is not loading</summary>

Try these steps:

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Close and reopen the app.
3. Check for an update in the **App Store** or **Play Store**.
4. If the problem continues, contact support.

</details>

<details>

<summary>My notification shows an error when I tap it</summary>

This is a known issue.

Dismiss the notification and open the related section of the app manually.

If possible, contact support with the exact error message.

</details>

### Rewards and referrals

<details>

<summary>I referred a friend but did not get credit</summary>

Make sure your friend used your specific referral link during sign-up.

Referral credit only activates after your friend gets their virtual card.

If they signed up without your link, the referral cannot be applied later.

Contact support with your friend's email address if you need the team to investigate.

</details>
