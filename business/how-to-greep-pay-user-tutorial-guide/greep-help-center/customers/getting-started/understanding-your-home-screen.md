# Understanding Your Home Screen

The Greep Pay home screen is your main app dashboard.

Use it to check your wallet balance, send money, fund your wallet, manage virtual cards, and view account updates.

### Balance and quick actions

* **Wallet Balance** shows your available Greep Pay wallet balance.
* **Fund Wallet** adds money through supported wallet funding options.
* **Send** lets you send money to another Greep Pay user.
* **Scan** lets you make QR code payments when available.

### Cards and accounts

* **Virtual Card(s)** opens your virtual card details, balance, and settings.
* **Account** gives access to supported virtual account options, including USD, GBP, and EUR accounts.

### Activity and updates

* **Notifications** shows transaction alerts, account updates, and announcements.
* **Rewards** shows your XP, badges, cashback balance, and leaderboard rank. Read [Greep Pay Rewards](../greep-pay-rewards.md).

### Explore more services

* **Explore** opens more Greep Pay services, including marketplace, events, and vendors.

{% hint style="info" %}
Some options may depend on your account status or verification level. Complete [KYC verification](how-to-complete-kyc-verification.md) to unlock more features.
{% endhint %}
