# Greep Pay Rewards

Earn rewards when you use Greep Pay for payments, card activity, referrals, and account actions.

The Greep Pay rewards system gives you **XP**, **cashback**, badges, and leaderboard progress for eligible activity.

**Related terms:** XP, Cashback, Quests, Referrals, Badges, Leaderboard, Reward Tasks

{% hint style="info" %}
Open the **Rewards** tab in the app to track your **XP**, **cashback balance**, **badges**, and **leaderboard rank**.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Referral rewards activate after the referred user gets their virtual card.

Sign-up alone does not complete the referral requirement.
{% endhint %}

### How Greep Pay rewards work

The more you use Greep Pay, the more you can earn.

Each reward task has its own **XP** and **cashback** value.

Available tasks can change over time.

You only see tasks that are currently available to your account.

### Greep Pay reward tasks

#### App usage reward tasks

You can earn rewards for these account and product actions:

* **Card Registration** — activate your virtual card to earn **120 XP** and **0.5 cashback**
* **Receive Your First Payment** — receive money from another Greep Pay user to earn **100 XP** and **0.02 cashback**
* **KYC Verification** — complete verification to earn **100 XP** and **0.5 cashback**
* **Your First Card Payment** — make your first card payment to earn **200 XP** and **0.2 cashback**
* **Fund Your Card** — add money to your card wallet to earn **60 XP** and **0.1 cashback**

#### Transaction reward tasks

You can also earn rewards by completing payment and transfer tasks:

* **Send Your First GreepPay Payment** — send money to another user to earn **105 XP** and **0.03 cashback**
* **Make 2 GreepPay Payments Today** — complete two payments in one day to earn **140 XP** and **0.1 cashback**
* **Send & Receive** — complete the required send and receive activity to earn **1200 XP** and **1.5 cashback**
* **Spend 5 times with your Card this week** — complete five card payments in one week to earn **250 XP** and **0.5 cashback**
* **Pay 3 Times with Your Card** — complete three card payments to earn **200 XP** and **1.2 cashback**

#### Referral reward tasks

Referral tasks can also reward both usage and growth:

* **Refer A Friend** — complete the referral goal to earn **1000 XP** and **2 cashback**
* **Refer a friend** — share your invite link with five people to earn **300 XP** and **0.25 cashback**

For a referral to count, the invited user must use your referral link and get their virtual card.

### Rewards, quests, and availability

Some reward tasks may be **active** or **inactive** at different times.

The rewards and quests shown in your app depend on which tasks are currently enabled.

### Check your XP, cashback, and leaderboard progress

Use the **Rewards** tab to:

* view your current XP
* check your cashback balance
* track badges and leaderboard rank
* review your rewards history

### What happens next

As your XP grows, your position on the leaderboard can change.

Keep using Greep Pay to complete quests, earn cashback, build XP, and improve your rank.
