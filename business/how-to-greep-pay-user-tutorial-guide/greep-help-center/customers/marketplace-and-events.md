# Marketplace & Events

Use **Explore** to buy products and event tickets in Greep Pay.

This flow lets you pay from your wallet and complete purchases in the app.

{% hint style="info" %}
Event tickets on GreepPay can be cheaper than buying at the door.

Check the event listing for the current price difference.
{% endhint %}

### How to buy on the marketplace

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open Explore

Open the GreepPay app.

Tap **Explore** from the bottom navigation.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Browse vendors and products

Browse the available vendors and products.

Select the product you want to buy.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Review the order

Tap **Buy**.

Check the product details before you continue.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Confirm payment

Confirm the payment from your GreepPay wallet.

Make sure your wallet balance covers the order.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wait for fulfilment

After payment succeeds, the vendor is notified.

The vendor then fulfils your order.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### How to buy event tickets

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open Events

Open the GreepPay app.

Tap **Explore** and select **Events**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find the event

Browse the available events.

Select the event you want to attend.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose your ticket

Select your ticket type and quantity.

Review the event details and pricing.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Buy the ticket

Tap **Buy Tickets**.

Confirm payment from your GreepPay wallet.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Access your ticket

After payment succeeds, your ticket is saved in the app.

Show the ticket at the event entrance.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

Your purchase appears in the app after payment succeeds.

If you need to add money first, go to [Wallet & Top-Up](wallet-and-top-up/).
