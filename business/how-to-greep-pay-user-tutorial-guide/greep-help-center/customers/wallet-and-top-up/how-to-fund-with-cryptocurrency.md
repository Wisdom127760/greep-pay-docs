# How to Fund with Cryptocurrency

Use this flow to add funds to your wallet from the **Crypto** tab.

Start from the **Add Money** screen.

**Step 1: Open the Crypto tab**

* Tap **"Add Money"**.
* Select the **Crypto** tab.

**Step 2: Choose the currency**

* Open the **Currency** list.
* Select the crypto you want to deposit, such as **USDT** or **USDC**.

**Step 3: Choose the network**

* Open the **Network** list.
* Select the matching network, such as **Ethereum** or **Tron**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Use the exact currency and network shown on the deposit screen.

If they do not match, your funds can be lost.
{% endhint %}

**Step 4: Review the deposit notice**

* Check the warning shown on the screen before you send funds.
* Do not send below **$10**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Any money sent to the displayed address is added directly to your wallet.

Always review the address details before you continue.
{% endhint %}

**Step 5: Copy or scan the address**

* Scan the QR code from your external wallet.
* Or copy the wallet address shown below the QR code.
* You can also use the share icon if needed.

**Step 6: Send the crypto**

* Open your external wallet or exchange.
* Paste the address or scan the QR code.
* Enter the amount you want to send.
* Confirm the transfer.

**Step 7: Wait for confirmation**

* Wait for the blockchain confirmation.
* Once confirmed, the funds are added directly to your wallet.
