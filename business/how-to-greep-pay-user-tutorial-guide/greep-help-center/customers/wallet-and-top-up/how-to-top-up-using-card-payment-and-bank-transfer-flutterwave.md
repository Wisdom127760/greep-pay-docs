# How to Top Up Using Card Payment and Bank Transfer (Flutterwave)

Start from the **Add Money** screen on the **Fiat** tab.

**Step 1: Enter your amount**

* Select **NGN** or your preferred fiat currency.
* Enter the amount you want to add.
* Tap **"Add Money"**.

**Step 2: Choose Flutterwave**

* In **Choose Deposit Method**, select **Flutterwave**.
* Review the displayed fee, usually **2–4%**.
* Tap **"Continue"**.

**Step 3: Select Card Payment**

* On **Fund with Flutterwave**, choose **Card Payment**.
* Tap **"Continue"** to open the secure payment screen.

{% hint style="info" %}
Flutterwave can also offer **Bank Transfer** on this screen.

Choose **Card Payment** to continue with this flow.
{% endhint %}

**Step 4: Review the payment amount**

* Check the total amount shown on the payment page.
* Review the transaction breakdown before you pay.

**Step 5: Enter your card details**

* Enter your card number.
* Enter the expiry date and **CVV**.
* Turn on **"Remember this card next time"** only if needed.

**Step 6: Complete the payment**

* Tap **"Pay NGN..."**.
* Complete any required card verification.

**Step 7: Wait for confirmation**

* Wait for the payment to be approved.
* Once successful, the funds appear in your wallet balance.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Fees can apply when you fund with **Flutterwave**.

Always review the final amount before you tap **"Pay"**.
{% endhint %}
