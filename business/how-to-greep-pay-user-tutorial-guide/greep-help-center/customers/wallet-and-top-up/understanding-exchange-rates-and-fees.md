# Understanding Exchange Rates and Fees

Greep Pay shows your exchange rate and fees before you confirm.

This includes currency conversion fees, wallet top-up fees, and crypto network fees.

#### 💱 What is an exchange rate?

An exchange rate shows one currency's value against another.

**Example**

* **1 USD = 1,500 NGN**
* This means **$1** converts to **₦1,500**.

Exchange rates are not fixed.

They change with market movement and the exchanger you select.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important**

* Always review the **final amount after fees** before you confirm.
* Greep Pay shows charges before payment.
{% endhint %}

#### 💸 What fees can apply?

You may see these fees during a top-up or conversion:

* **Service fee** — charged by the platform or exchanger
* **Conversion fee** — charged when you change currencies
* **Network fee** — charged for blockchain transactions
* **Payment provider fee** — charged for some card or bank transfer payments

All fees appear before you confirm the transaction.

#### 📊 Why exchange rates and fees change

Rates can vary depending on:

* The **exchanger** you select
* The **currency pair**, such as **USD → NGN** or **USDT → USD**
* The **payment method**, such as card, bank transfer, or crypto
* **Market demand and supply**
* **Transaction speed**
* **Blockchain network conditions** for crypto

#### ✅ How to avoid unexpected charges

* Review the full amount before you tap **Confirm**.
* Compare available exchangers when options appear.
* Check the currency pair before you continue.
* Expect network fees to change during busy crypto periods.
