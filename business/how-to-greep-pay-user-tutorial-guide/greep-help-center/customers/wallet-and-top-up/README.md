# Wallet & Top-Up

Add money to your wallet from the home screen.

Tap **"Fund"** on your wallet card to get started.

**Related terms:** Fund Wallet, Add Money, Bank Transfer, P2P, Crypto Deposit, Top-Up, Exchange Rates

### Video tutorial

Watch how to top up your Greep Pay wallet:

{% embed url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GALsLlSII9o" %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Your default currency must match the currency you want to add.

If your default currency is **USD**, you cannot fund with **NGN** until you switch it.
{% endhint %}

#### Choose a funding method

Greep Pay supports three wallet funding options:

1. **Bank Transfer**
   * View your unique Greep Pay account details.
   * Send money from any supported bank account.
   * Funds usually arrive within minutes.
2. **P2P (Peer-to-Peer)**
   * Browse offers from verified traders.
   * Compare rates and available amounts.
   * Complete payment through the in-app chat flow.
   * Read [How to Fund Your Wallet with NGN](how-to-fund-your-wallet-with-p2p.md).
3. **Cryptocurrency**
   * Choose a supported asset.
   * Select the correct network.
   * Send funds to your deposit address.
   * Read [How to Fund with Cryptocurrency](how-to-fund-with-cryptocurrency.md).

#### How wallet top-up works

**Step 1: Open Add Money**

* From the **Home** screen, tap **"Fund"** on your wallet card.
* Select **"Fund with African Currency"**, **"Fund with NGN"**, or your preferred funding option.

**Step 2: Enter your amount**

* Enter the amount you want to add.
* Review the rate and any fees before you continue.

**Step 3: Complete the payment**

* Follow the steps for your selected method.
* Use the provided account details, trader instructions, or crypto address.

**Step 4: Confirm the deposit**

* Wait for the **"Deposit Successful"** message.
* Check your wallet balance on the home screen.
* Review the transaction in **Recent Transactions** if you need a record.

#### Rates and fees

Rates and fees vary by currency, method, and provider.

See [Understanding Exchange Rates and Fees](understanding-exchange-rates-and-fees.md) for details.
