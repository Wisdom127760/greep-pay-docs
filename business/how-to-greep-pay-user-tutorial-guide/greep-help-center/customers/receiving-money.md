# Receiving Money

Receive money from another Greep Pay user from the **Receive** flow.

Use this option when someone wants to pay you inside Greep Pay.

**Related terms:** Receive Money, Username, QR Code, Greep Pay Link, Wallet Transfer, Payment Request

{% hint style="info" %}
You can receive money by sharing your **username**, **QR code**, or **Greep Pay link**.

When the transfer succeeds, the money appears in your wallet.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the receive flow

Open the Greep Pay app.

Tap **Receive**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose how to share your details

Share your **username**, **QR code**, or **Greep Pay link** with the sender.

Use the option that is easiest for them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Let the sender complete the transfer

The sender can search for your username or scan your QR code.

They then confirm the payment in their app.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wait for the money to arrive

Once the transfer is complete, the money is added to your wallet.

You receive a notification when the funds arrive.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check the transaction details

Open your wallet or transaction history to review the payment.

You can confirm the sender name and amount there.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

After you receive the money, you can keep it in your wallet or use it for payments and transfers.

If you want to send money too, read [Transfer to GreepPay Users](sending-money/transfer-to-greeppay-users.md).
