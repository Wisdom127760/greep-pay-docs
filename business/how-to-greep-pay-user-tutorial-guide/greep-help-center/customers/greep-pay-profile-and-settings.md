# Greep Pay Profile and Settings

Open your **Greep Pay Profile** to manage your personal details and account settings in one place.

Use this screen to update your profile info, check your KYC level, manage delivery addresses, change your default currency, update security settings, and contact customer support.

### Video tutorial

Watch how to set up your profile and account settings:

{% embed url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGO_mX1YTAM" %}

### Open your Greep Pay profile

Tap the **Profile** icon from the home screen.

### What you can manage in your Greep Pay profile

#### Personal Info

Update your basic Greep Pay account details, such as your name, email address, phone number, and profile photo.

#### KYC Levels

Check your current KYC verification tier and complete more verification if you need higher transaction limits.

#### Delivery Address

Add, update, or remove saved delivery addresses from your Greep Pay profile.

#### Default Currency

Choose the main currency shown across your Greep Pay app experience.

#### Security Settings

Manage your transaction PIN, account password, and app passcode.

#### Customer Support

Open customer support options when you need help with your account, settings, or a transaction.

#### Log Out

Use **Log Out** when you want to sign out of the app on your device.

### Update personal info in Greep Pay

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open Personal Info

Go to **Profile**.

Select **Personal Info**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Make your changes

Update the details you want to edit.

Check that the information is correct before you save it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save the update

Tap **Save** or **Update**.

Enter your password if the app asks you to confirm the change.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Manage delivery addresses in your profile

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open Delivery Address

Go to **Profile**.

Select **Delivery Address**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Add or edit an address

Tap **Add Address** to save a new address.

If an address already exists, open it to update the details.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save the address

Enter the address details and save the change.

Set it as your default address if needed.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

Changes you make in your **Greep Pay Profile** apply to your account immediately or after confirmation.

For security changes, read [Security and Settings](security-and-settings/).
