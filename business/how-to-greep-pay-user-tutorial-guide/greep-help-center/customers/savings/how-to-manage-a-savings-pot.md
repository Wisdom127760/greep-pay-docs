# How to Manage a Savings Pot

Manage your **Savings Pot** from the **Savings** screen in Greep Pay.

Use this page to add money, withdraw available funds, review activity, and update pot settings.

{% include "../../../.gitbook/includes/savings-related-terms.md" %}

### Add money to a Savings Pot

To top up an existing pot:

1. Open **Savings**.
2. Select the pot you want to fund.
3. Tap **Add Money** or **Fund Pot**.
4. Enter the amount.
5. Confirm the transfer from your **Greep Pay wallet balance**.

### Withdraw from a Savings Pot

To move money back to your wallet:

1. Open **Savings**.
2. Select the pot.
3. Tap **Withdraw**.
4. Enter the amount you want to move.
5. Confirm the transfer.

{% hint style="info" %}
If your pot is locked, withdrawals may be restricted until the lock period ends or the pot conditions are met.
{% endhint %}

### View Savings Pot activity

Each pot can include an activity history for actions such as:

* deposits
* auto-save contributions
* round-up transfers
* withdrawals

Use the activity list to track how your pot balance changes over time.

### Update Savings Pot settings

Depending on your pot type and the options shown in the app, you may be able to update:

* the pot title
* the goal amount
* auto-save settings

Open the pot first, then check **Settings** or the available actions on that pot.
