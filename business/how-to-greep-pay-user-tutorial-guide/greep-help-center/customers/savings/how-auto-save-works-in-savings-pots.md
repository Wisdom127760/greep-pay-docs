# How Auto-Save Works in Savings Pots

**Auto-save** helps you grow a **Savings Pot** without making every transfer manually.

Use these options to save automatically from your regular spending activity.

{% include "../../../.gitbook/includes/savings-related-terms.md" %}

### Auto-save options

Greep Pay can show these auto-save options during pot setup or in pot settings:

* **Percentage of Payment**
* **Round-Up Payments**

### Percentage of Payment

With **Percentage of Payment**, a portion of each payment is moved into your selected pot automatically.

For example, if you set **5%** and spend **$20**, the app saves **$1** to your pot.

### Round-Up Payments

With **Round-Up Payments**, each spend is rounded up and the difference is saved to your pot.

For example, if you spend **$4.60**, the app can save **$0.40**.

### Turn auto-save on or off

To manage auto-save:

1. Open **Savings**.
2. Select your pot.
3. Open the pot settings or auto-save options.
4. Turn your preferred option on or off.
5. Save the change.

### What to check before you enable auto-save

Review the savings option carefully before you confirm.

Make sure you understand how often money moves into your pot and which transactions qualify.

{% hint style="info" %}
Available auto-save options can vary by pot type and current product availability.
{% endhint %}
