# How to withdraw from card to wallet

Move money from your Greep Pay virtual card back to your wallet from the **Cards** tab.

This helps you manage your virtual card balance and return unused funds to your wallet.

{% hint style="info" %}
The amount you can withdraw depends on your available card balance.

Keep a small extra balance on your card if you have pending charges or unsettled transactions.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open your virtual card

Go to the **Cards** tab in the app.

Select the virtual card you want to withdraw from.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Tap Withdraw

On the card screen, tap **Withdraw**.

This opens the card-to-wallet transfer flow.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter the withdrawal amount

Enter the amount you want to move from your virtual card to your wallet.

Review the amount before you continue.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Confirm the transfer

Confirm the withdrawal to send the money back to your wallet.

After the transfer succeeds, refresh the page if needed to see the updated balances.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

Once the transfer is complete, the money appears in your wallet balance.

If you want to add money again later, read [How to fund your card](how-to-fund-your-card.md).
