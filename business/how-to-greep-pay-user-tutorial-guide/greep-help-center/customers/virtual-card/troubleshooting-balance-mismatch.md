# Troubleshooting: Balance mismatch

If your Greep Pay virtual card balance looks wrong, the difference is usually caused by pending charges, delayed updates, or unsettled card transactions.

Use these checks to understand a virtual card balance mismatch before you try another payment or withdrawal.

{% hint style="info" %}
Your available balance can differ from the amount you expect for a short time.

This can happen after a recent top-up, withdrawal, refund, or merchant charge.
{% endhint %}

### Common reasons for a card balance mismatch

Your card balance may not match your expectation if:

* a recent card payment is still pending
* a merchant settles the final amount later
* cross-border or foreign transaction fees are added after payment
* a wallet-to-card top-up has not fully refreshed yet
* a card-to-wallet withdrawal is still processing

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Refresh your card balance

Open the **Cards** tab and select your virtual card.

Refresh the screen to check if the latest balance has updated.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Review recent card activity

Check your recent card payments, top-ups, and withdrawals.

Look for pending charges, reversals, or refunds that may still be processing.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check for delayed merchant charges

Some merchants first place a temporary charge, then settle the final amount later.

This can make your card balance look lower or different than expected.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check for extra international charges

Cross-border fees, currency conversion, or foreign transaction charges can be added after the original payment.

Keep a small extra balance on your card to cover these adjustments.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Compare your card balance with your wallet transfers

If you recently funded your card or withdrew money back to your wallet, confirm that the transfer completed successfully.

Wait a moment and refresh again if the balances do not match yet.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

If your card needs more balance, read [How to fund your card](how-to-fund-your-card.md).

If you want to move unused money out of the card, read [How to withdraw from card to wallet](how-to-withdraw-from-card-to-wallet.md).
