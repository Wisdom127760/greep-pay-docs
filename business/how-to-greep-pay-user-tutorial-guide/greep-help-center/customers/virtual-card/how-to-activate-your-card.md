# How to activate your card

Activate your Greep Pay virtual card from the **Cards** tab.

You can choose **Mastercard** or **Visa** during setup.

{% hint style="info" %}
The card creation fee is **$3.99**.

Make sure your wallet has enough balance before you start.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the Cards tab

From the bottom navigation, tap **Cards**.

On the **Virtual Cards** screen, tap **Get Card**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Unclaimed Virtual Cards.png" alt="Virtual Cards screen with the Get Card button" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose your card type

Select the virtual card you want to create:

* **Mastercard**
* **Visa**

Each card costs **$3.99** to create.

Tap **Continue** to move to the next step.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Complete card creation

Review your selection and confirm the payment.

Once payment succeeds, your virtual card is created instantly.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Start using your card

After activation, your new card appears on the **Virtual Cards** screen.

From here, you can:

* Tap **Add Money** to fund the card
* Tap **Details** to view card information
* Tap **Lock** to freeze or unfreeze the card
* Tap **Settings** to manage card options


{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

After activation, you can fund your card and use it for online payments, subscriptions, and international purchases.

Next, read [How to fund your card](how-to-fund-your-card.md).
