# How to add to Apple Pay / Google Pay

Add your Greep Pay virtual card to **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay** from the **Cards** tab.

This lets you use your card for faster checkout, digital wallet payments, and supported contactless purchases.

{% hint style="info" %}
Apple Pay and Google Pay availability can depend on your device, region, and card support.

Make sure your phone already supports the wallet you want to use.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
This is a **US-based virtual card**.

If your phone region or wallet region is set to another country, Apple Pay or Google Pay may not let you add the card.

If setup fails, open your phone settings and switch your device region to the **United States**, then try again.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the Cards tab

From the bottom navigation, tap **Cards**.

Select the virtual card you want to add to your digital wallet.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Start the wallet setup

Open your card options or settings.

Tap **Add to Apple Pay** or **Add to Google Pay** if the option appears.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Review your card details

Check that the selected card is correct.

Follow the on-screen prompts to continue the wallet setup.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Complete verification

Finish any verification steps required during setup.

Once approved, your virtual card is added to Apple Pay or Google Pay.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Start paying with your digital wallet

Use your wallet for faster checkout on supported apps, websites, and contactless payment terminals.

Always make sure your card has enough balance before you pay.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### What happens next

If you need to check your card information first, read [How to view card details](how-to-view-card-details.md).

If a wallet payment does not work, check [Troubleshooting: Card declined](troubleshooting-card-declined.md).
