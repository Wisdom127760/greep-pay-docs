# A tour of your dashboard

Open the menu to find these.

## Good to know

- **Dashboard:** your business at a glance: revenue, transactions, customers and conversion.
- **Products, Storefront, Payment Links, Invoices, Link-in-bio, Link Shortener:** everything for selling and getting paid.
- **Wallet:** balances, transaction history and settlements. Receive or send money here.
- **Accounts:** bank details your customers use to pay you.
- **Savings:** put money aside in pots. **Exchange Rates:** set your buy and sell rates.
- **Help:** contact our support team. **Settings:** your profile, tools, integrations, API keys, notifications and payment settings.
- Marketing, Loyalty Program, Referrals, Affiliate Marketing, Reviews and Analytics are marked **Coming Soon**.
