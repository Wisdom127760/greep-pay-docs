# Choose your tools

You decide which tools appear in your sidebar.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Tools**.
2. Tick or untick the tools you want. They are grouped as Sell, Grow and Understand customers.
3. Press **Save Changes**.

- Available now: Storefront, Payment Links and Link in Bio. The rest are marked Coming Soon.
