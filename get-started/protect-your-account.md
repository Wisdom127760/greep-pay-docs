# Protect your account

Your account holds your money, so look after it.

## Do

- Use a password only you know, and change it if you think someone else has seen it.
- Keep your **transaction PIN** private. It approves sensitive transactions.
- Keep your **secret API key** out of any public website code.

## Don't

{% hint style="danger" %}
Never share your password, PIN or secret API key with anyone, including our support team. We will never ask for them.
{% endhint %}

## Change them

See [Change your password or transaction PIN](../account-and-security/change-your-password-or-transaction-pin.md).
