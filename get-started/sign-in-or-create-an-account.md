# Sign in or create an account

## Sign in

1. Go to your Greep Pay merchant dashboard.
2. Choose **Continue with Google**, or type your **email address** and press **Continue**.
3. Follow the steps on screen. [confirm: what comes after the email step, such as a password or a code]

## Create an account

If you are new, press **Create account** under the sign-in form. [confirm: what creating an account asks for]

## If you can't sign in

1. Check you are using the email your business account was created with.
2. If you normally use **Continue with Google**, use it again with the same Google account.
3. Check for typos and try again.

{% hint style="info" %}
Still stuck? Send us the email on your account and a screenshot of any message you see. Please don't send your password or PIN.
{% endhint %}
