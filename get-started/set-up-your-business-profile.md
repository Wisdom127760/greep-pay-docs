# Set up your business profile

There are two profiles: yours, and your business's. Both are in Settings.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Account Profile** to change your photo, full name and phone number. Press **Edit** next to a detail.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Business Profile** to change your business logo, name, email, category and bio, then press **Edit** and save.

## Good to know

- Your business name and bio are what customers see, so keep them accurate.
