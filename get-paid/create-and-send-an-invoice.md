# Create and send an invoice

An invoice lists what you are charging and gives your client a secure link to pay.

## Steps

1. Open **Invoices**, then press **Create Invoice**.
2. Under **Bill to**, choose a saved client or enter a new one (name, phone and email).
3. Check the invoice number, currency, reference and the issue and due dates.
4. Add your **line items**: what you are charging, the quantity and the rate. A discount and tax are optional, and the total is worked out for you.
5. Check how your client can pay: card, bank transfer, GreepPay wallet or crypto.
6. Press **Save**. Then email it to your client or download the PDF.

## Good to know

- Save the invoice first. The email and PDF buttons only work after it is saved.
- For an invoice in naira, the money settles into your GreepPay NGN wallet.
- The Invoices page shows how many are outstanding and overdue. Invoices are listed as Draft, Sent, Paid or Cancelled.
