# Request money from your wallet

A payment request asks a customer to pay you a set amount in the currency you choose.

## Steps

1. Open **Wallet** and press **Receive**.
2. Choose **Fiat** or **Crypto**.
3. Pick the currency. You can search the list.
4. Press **Continue** and follow the steps to create and share the request.

## Good to know

- The list includes naira, Kenyan shilling, Ghanaian cedi, South African rand, US dollar, euro and the CFA francs, among others. Which currencies work for your business may differ, so ask us if you're unsure.
