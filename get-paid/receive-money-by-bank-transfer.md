# Receive money by bank transfer

Your dashboard gives you bank details in several currencies. Share them with your customers so they can pay you by transfer.

## Steps

1. Open **Accounts** in your dashboard.
2. Choose the currency: Nigeria (NGN), United States (USD) or Europe (EUR). The United Kingdom (GBP) account is coming soon.
3. Share the details shown with your customer.
4. Ask them to copy every detail exactly as shown, including the **reference** if one is listed.

## Good to know

- **Nigeria (NGN):** bank name, account name and account number.
- **United States (USD), by ACH:** beneficiary name, routing number, account number, address and a deposit message.
- **Europe (EUR), by SEPA:** account holder name, bank name, BIC, IBAN and a SEPA reference.
- The account name on euro and dollar accounts isn't your own business name. That is normal.
- A payment sent with wrong details can be delayed or returned.
